Poké Balls

Learn about the different Poké Balls available at Poké Ball Emporium.

Poké Ball

The standard Poké Ball used by trainers to catch Pokémon. It is the most common Poké Ball.

Great Ball

The Great Ball is an upgraded Poké Ball that has a better chance of catching Pokémon.

Ultra Ball

The Ultra Ball is more powerful than the Great Ball and has a higher chance of catching Pokémon.

Master Ball

The Master Ball is an extremely powerful Poké Ball that can catch a Pokémon without fail.

Premier Ball

The Premier Ball is a special Poké Ball with a unique white design.

Quick Ball

The Quick Ball is especially useful when it is used at the beginning of a battle.

Dusk Ball

The Dusk Ball is especially useful in dark places, such as caves, and at night.

Net Ball

The Net Ball is especially effective for catching Bug-type and Water-type Pokémon.

Luxury Ball

The Luxury Ball is designed to make Pokémon feel more comfortable after being caught.
