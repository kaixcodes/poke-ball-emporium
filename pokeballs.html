Poké Balls

Each of our Pokémon balls are well selected from a wide variety of fully test and operational Pokéballs Our Poké Ball collection has many different types for trainers. Each Poké Ball has its own special features and uses.

1. Poké Ball

The standard Poké Ball used by trainers to catch Pokémon. It is the basic Poké Ball and is commonly used by trainers.

2. Great Ball

The Great Ball is an upgraded version of the regular Poké Ball. It has a higher chance of catching Pokémon.

3. Ultra Ball

The Ultra Ball is a powerful Poké Ball with an even higher chance of catching Pokémon than a Great Ball.

4. Master Ball

The Master Ball is an extremely powerful Poké Ball. It can catch a Pokémon without fail.

5. Quick Ball

The Quick Ball is most effective when it is used at the beginning of a battle.

6. Premier Ball

The Premier Ball is a special white Poké Ball. It has the same basic catching ability as a regular Poké Ball.

7. Net Ball

The Net Ball is especially effective for catching Bug-type and Water-type Pokémon.

8. Luxury Ball

The Luxury Ball is designed to make Pokémon feel more comfortable after being caught.

9. Dusk Ball

The Dusk Ball is especially useful in dark places such as caves and is also useful at night.
